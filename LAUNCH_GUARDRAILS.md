# TigerClaw.io launch guardrails

Use this file before public copy, checkout, legal, footer, or metadata changes.
It aligns TigerClaw.io with TheGoods.ai without merging their audiences.

## Brandmap alignment

TigerClaw.io is the customer product site. TheGoods.ai is the company and
platform site. Keep this separation clear on every public page.

- TigerClaw.io message: Tiger Claw is your autonomous AI follow-up agent.
- TigerClaw.io audience: independent distributors, affiliates, social sellers,
  and relationship-driven operators.
- TigerClaw.io product promise: help users remember prospect details, draft
  better replies, handle common objections, organize follow-up, and keep serious
  conversations moving toward the next step.
- TheGoods.ai message: The Goods builds AI agent infrastructure for operators,
  agencies, teams, and selected platform conversations.
- TheGoods.ai platform language must not be moved into TigerClaw.io customer
  copy.

## Public copy rules

Do not name specific network marketing, direct selling, affiliate, social
selling, or marketplace companies in public copy, metadata, checkout copy, or
disclaimers.

Use broad, neutral disclaimer language:

- Tiger Claw is an independent software tool.
- It is not produced, approved, sponsored, endorsed, or recommended by any
  company, platform, marketplace, network, affiliate program, direct selling
  company, network marketing company, or social-selling company.
- Results are not guaranteed.
- Users are responsible for their own company, platform, program, privacy,
  advertising, and communication compliance.

## AI responsibility rule

Use this rule anywhere the site describes AI-generated content:

> AI may draft notes, replies, summaries, and guidance. The user reviews,
> edits, approves, sends, and stays compliant.

Do not imply Tiger Claw sends messages, makes claims, books meetings, recruits
people, or produces business outcomes without human review.

## Checkout guardrails

Checkout copy must describe an autonomous AI follow-up agent subscription, not recruiting
automation.

- Keep the price clear: `$147/month`.
- Keep cancellation clear: cancel any time; access continues through the paid
  period.
- Do not promise sales, signups, commissions, rank advancement, income,
  conversion rates, or customer acquisition.
- Do not suggest the Stripe checkout page is affiliated with any third-party
  company or program.
- Stripe dashboard copy must be updated manually when site copy changes.

## Required customer paths

Before launch, verify that these paths are present and linked from the footer:

- Contact
- FAQ
- Privacy Policy
- Terms of Service
- Results Disclaimer
- Support Promise
- Cancellation

The cancellation path may point to the refund and cancellation policy if that
section clearly explains how to cancel.
